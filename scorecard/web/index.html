        Quick answers

        What do I get from the scorecard?

        Five scores out of 5, with the evidence behind each, in about a minute. Enter your email and you also get the three fixes that should move pipeline fastest.

        What does it read?

        Your public homepage: the headline, the opening text, numbers and quotes, calls to action and LinkedIn links. It does not see your CRM, ads or outbound results, so treat it as a place to start looking, not a full audit.

        Why do you ask for my email?

        The scores are free and need nothing. The fix list is where we ask for an email, so we can follow up about it.

        What if my page cannot be scored?

        Pages that need heavy JavaScript to show anything, and pages not in English, cannot be read reliably. We tell you that rather than guess, and you can still book the call.

        What happens on the 20-minute call?

        We go through your scorecard and the three fixes, work out which part to fix first, and tell you honestly whether we are the right people to do it.
